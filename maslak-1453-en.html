Maslak 1453 Apartments for Sale and Rent

Whether you are looking for an apartment, residence or office in Maslak 1453, or want to sell or rent out your own unit, you are in the right place. Referans Maslak, an English-speaking real estate agency in Maslak, has its office inside the project, at Block T4b No:62. Since 2018 we have personally managed purchases, sales and rentals in the project.

Why Maslak 1453?

Ağaoğlu Maslak 1453 is a large-scale development close to the Maslak business district, combining apartments, residences, offices and retail space. With its on-site amenities, green areas and city and forest views, it is one of the preferred projects both for living and for rental income.

For buyers

We identify the units that fit your budget and purpose (living, rental income or investment) and assess how block, floor, aspect and view affect the price. Drawing on our civil engineering background, we also review the technical condition of the unit with you, and follow the title deed and official procedures through to completion.

For tenants

We shortlist furnished and unfurnished apartments and offices according to your needs, and manage viewings, negotiation and the lease agreement.

For owners selling or letting

Valuation based on current market data, professional listings and matching with the right buyer or tenant. For let units we can also take over the lease agreement, rent collection and tenant management.

International investors

We advise in English and follow title deed, residence permit and Turkish citizenship by investment processes from start to finish.
